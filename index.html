Long-Term Population Distribution ?

A steady state is a population distribution that remains unchanged after applying the migration matrix: M P* = P*, which can be written M P* = 1 · P*. The steady-state vector corresponds to eigenvalue 1. Migration does not stop at steady state; the distribution simply stops changing.

Limitations

  • Migration rates are assumed constant.
  • Births, deaths and external migration are not included.
  • Economic conditions, housing and government policy are not modeled.

The model is a simplified mathematical framework, not an exact real-world forecast.
